# Signature Concepts — Delta Light

## 1) Light as Architecture (SELECTED)
- Hero: dark architectural scene, central glowing chandelier, rays + gradual illumination.
- Scroll: cinematic continuous journey, pinned product story DESIGN→DETAIL→MATERIAL→LIGHT→SPACE.
- Product: experienced in rooms (RoomVisualizer), not generic cards.
- Visual: deep black #080808, warm gold #C9A85D, editorial asymmetry.
- Conversion: WhatsApp-first (floating, product, cart, showroom).

## 2) Showroom Night Walk (runner-up)
- Hero: virtual night showroom corridor.
- Rejected: heavier WebGL cost, weaker mobile feasibility.

## 3) Editorial Catalog Luxe (runner-up)
- Hero: magazine grid.
- Rejected: less immersive, weaker brand differentiation.

Selection criteria: brand fit, product visibility, UX, conversion, performance, mobile — concept 1 wins all.
